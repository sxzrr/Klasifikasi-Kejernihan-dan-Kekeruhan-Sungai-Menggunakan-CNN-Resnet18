💡 KEY INSIGHTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WHY SIMPLE AUGMENTATION WORKS FOR WATER:

1. Water is rotation-invariant
   └─ River looks same whether tilted ±30°

2. Lighting varies naturally
   └─ Different times of day, weather conditions
   └─ Color jitter simulates this well

3. Camera position matters
   └─ Higher/lower, angled differently
   └─ Perspective transforms simulate this

4. Water surfaces ripple
   └─ Blur effects mimic natural ripple variations

5. Mirror symmetry common
   └─ Horizontal/vertical flips realistic for rivers

RESULT: High-quality augmentation that respects physics!
